Delete your account

For the Long Way Round app. You only have an account if you chose Continue with Google. The app works without one.

In the app

  1. Open Long Way Round and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is removed straight away and the app signs you out.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, write to killoldesai@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We will delete it and reply to confirm.

What is removed

  • Your account, with the email, name and profile picture from Google.
  • The link between your account and your install record. The anonymous install record itself (a random id, platform and app version) stays as a count and no longer points to you.

What is not on our servers

Your focus sessions, notes, goals, stamps and postcards are stored only on your phone. They are not deleted by deleting your account. Remove them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, and export a copy first from Settings if you want to keep it.

See the privacy policy for the full picture.
