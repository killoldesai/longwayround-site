How Long Way Round works

Focus time becomes distance. Distance becomes stops. Stops become postcards and stamps.

1. Choose what you are focusing on

Pick a focus area such as Study, Reading or Work. Each one can remember a little about what you are doing, like the book you are reading or the one task you want to finish.

2. Start the timer

Choose Pomodoro, countdown or stopwatch, then press start. Your traveller sets off along the road you picked. While the timer runs the scene keeps moving, and it pauses when you pause.

3. Every minute adds distance

On the Amalfi Coast Road, 20 hours of focus walk the full 50 km, so a classic 25 minute session covers roughly 1 km. Other roads scale the same way.

4. Reach a stop, collect a postcard

When you pass a town the app unlocks its postcard, a short story and a true fact about the place, and a stamp in your passport. Browse them any time in the Passport tab.

5. Stop early, rest at the inn

Real days are messy. If you stop before the timer ends, the distance you walked is kept and the traveller rests until you start again. You choose whether very short sessions count.

6. See how you are doing

Stats show your week against a daily goal, your streak with a grace day, time by focus area and the total distance walked. History lets you fix any session.
