Frequently asked questions

What is Long Way Round?

Long Way Round is a focus timer for Android and iOS. While the timer runs, a small traveller walks a real road such as the Amalfi Coast. Every stop earns a postcard and a passport stamp.

What does it cost?

The timer, every focus area and the Amalfi Coast road are free. A Pro plan with more roads and deeper stats is planned and is not on sale yet. Nothing in the app asks for payment today.

Is it a Pomodoro timer?

Yes. It offers Pomodoro, countdown and stopwatch modes, with Gentle (15 minutes focus, 3 rest), Classic (25 and 5) and Deep (50 and 10) rhythms, or your own lengths. Every fourth rest can be longer.

What happens if I stop a session early?

The distance you walked is kept. If your timer rules say sessions shorter than a minimum do not count, the app tells you before you stop. In strict mode an early stop does not count the session.

Does the timer keep running when the screen is off?

The timer is worked out from the time you started, so it stays correct when the app is in the background or closed. An alert is scheduled for when your session or break ends.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works offline without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps your account when you change phones.

Where is my data stored?

Focus sessions, notes, goals, stamps and postcards stay on your phone. The app sends an anonymous install record (a random id, the platform and the app version) so installs can be counted. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account or my data?

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. Focus data lives only on your phone, so clearing the app data or uninstalling removes it. You can export it first as CSV or JSON.

Can I fix a session I forgot to start?

Yes. History lets you add, edit or delete past sessions, and deletes can be undone.

Which roads can I walk?

The Amalfi Coast Road (50 km, free), the Camino Portugués (240 km) and the Great Ocean Road (243 km). More roads are planned.

Which phones does it run on?

Android first, with iOS to follow.
