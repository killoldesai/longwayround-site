A practice timer for learning a language

Choose the language and the skill you are practising, then keep the daily habit going.

What the Language area keeps

  • Your language and the skill for today: Vocabulary, Listening, Speaking, Reading, Writing or Grammar.
  • New words. Log how many you met in a session.
  • Nudges. Speaking out loud counts. Other skills suggest pairing them with speaking or writing later.

Little and often

Languages reward regular short sessions more than rare long ones. Grace days keep a streak alive over one missed day, so a busy Tuesday does not end the habit.
