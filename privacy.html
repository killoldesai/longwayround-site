Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. Applies to the Long Way Round mobile app and this website.

Long Way Round is a focus timer that works offline and needs no account. This page says plainly what is kept on your phone, what leaves it, and what you can do about it.

What stays on your phone

  • Your focus sessions, notes, focus areas, book, project, subject and task details, goals, settings, passport stamps and postcards.
  • This data is never sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.
  • You can export it yourself as CSV or JSON from Settings.

What the app sends to us

WhatWhenWhy
An install record: a random install id made on first launch, the platform (Android or iOS), the app version, the operating system version and a launch countEach time the app opensTo count installs and active devices and to see which versions are in use. It is not linked to you unless you sign in.
Your Google account email, name and profile pictureOnly if you choose Continue with GoogleTo create and show your account. Your Google password is never seen by the app or by us.

If you sign in, we link your install record to your account so we can count signed-in installs. We use no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or sites.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. It stores the install records and, for signed-in people, the account details above.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you use it, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Expo (EAS) builds the app. It does not receive your usage data.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits under its own policy. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local alerts on your phone for when a session or break ends. They are created on the device and no notification content is sent anywhere.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account. Everything works, and only the install record above is sent.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings, Account, Delete account. See how to delete your account, including a way to ask by email.
  • Ask what we hold or ask us to delete the install record. Email us with your install id if you know it, or the date you installed, and we will remove what we can match.

Children

The app is a general focus timer and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has signed in, write to us and we will delete the account.

Retention and security

Install records are kept while the app is in use, so we can see trends over time. Account data is kept until you delete your account. Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with row-level security, so signed-out requests cannot read it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and say so in the app's release notes.
