Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. Applies to the Long Way Round app for Android and iOS and to this website.

Long Way Round is a focus timer that works offline and needs no account. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to match the Google Play data safety form and the App Store privacy details for the app.

At a glance

  • Your focus sessions, notes, goals, stamps and postcards stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • We receive an anonymous install record (a random id, platform, app version, operating system version, launch count) so we can count installs and see which versions are in use.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the account details they share with us, so you have an account.
  • We do not run ads, we do not sell personal data, we do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account in the app, and erase the data on your phone, at any time.

Who we are

Long Way Round is built and run by an independent developer. For privacy questions or requests write to killoldesai@gmail.com. For people in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we are the controller of the personal data described below.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Focus sessions, notes, focus areas, your book, project, subject and task details, goals, settings, passport stamps and postcards, and the traveller you designed.
  • This data is stored on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing the app's data, using Settings, Your data, Erase data on this phone, or uninstalling removes it.
  • You can export it yourself as CSV or JSON from Settings.

What we do not collect

Location, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera, health or fitness data, financial or payment information, browsing history, advertising identifiers, and the content of your sessions or notes.

Why we use your data, and our legal basis

  • To provide your account (sign in, sign out, delete). Basis: performing our agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To count installs, understand versions and keep the service secure. Basis: our legitimate interests in running and improving the app, which we have weighed against your privacy by keeping the data minimal and unlinked to you unless you sign in (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object at any time, see Your rights.
  • To meet legal duties, such as responding to lawful requests and handling deletion requests. Basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it. If the app or its business is ever transferred, the data would move to the new owner under this policy.

International transfers

Our servers are in Japan, and our providers may process data in other countries including the United States. Where personal data from the EEA or UK is transferred, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Account details: until you delete your account, which removes them straight away.
  • Install records: kept while the app is in use so we can see trends. When you delete your account the record is unlinked from you and stays only as an anonymous count. You can ask us to delete it, see below.
  • Technical logs: kept for a short time by our hosting provider, then discarded.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct it, or delete it,
  • object to or restrict our use of it, and withdraw consent where we rely on it,
  • move your data to another service,
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

How to use them. The quickest way is in the app: Settings, Account, Delete account removes your account, and Settings, Your data, Erase data on this phone clears everything stored on the device. Our delete account page explains this and how to ask by email. For anything else write to killoldesai@gmail.com. We will confirm that it is you, usually by writing to the address on the account, and reply within 30 days. If you cannot sign in, tell us the email on the account or the date you installed and we will do what we can to match it.

Complaints. If you are in the EEA or UK you can complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents

In the last 12 months we collected the categories of personal information listed under Data we collect: identifiers (install id, account id, email, name), internet or device activity (app and OS version, launch count) and, if you sign in, a profile picture. We collected it from you and from Google or Apple at your request, for the purposes above. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

Sign in with Apple and Google

Signing in is optional and the app is fully usable without it. If you use Sign in with Apple you can hide your email, and then we only receive Apple's private relay address. You can also stop sharing at any time in your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple, and deleting your account in the app ends the link on our side and tells Apple to remove it. For Google, you can review and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Permissions and notifications

The app asks to send notifications so it can tell you when a session or break ends. These are local notifications created on your device, and no notification content is sent to us. The app also uses an exact-alarm permission on Android so that alert arrives on time. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.

Children

Long Way Round is a general-audience focus timer and is not directed at children under 13, nor are we targeting the Designed for Families or Kids categories. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with row-level security, so people who are not signed in cannot read it and signed-in people can read only their own account data. No system is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we collect to a minimum.

Cookies, tracking and Do Not Track

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising scripts. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not respond to Do Not Track signals because there is nothing to turn off.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and mention it in the app's release notes. Earlier versions are available on request.

Contact

Questions or requests: killoldesai@gmail.com.
