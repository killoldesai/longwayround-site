Amalfi Coast Road: 50 km, one session at a time

Cliff villages, lemon terraces and a road that never stops turning. In Long Way Round the road takes about 20 hours of focus to walk from Positano to Vietri sul Mare.

Free road Italy. 11 stops. Distances between stops are approximate and drawn from public sources.

Stops and postcards

Positano 0 km

Positano at first light. Houses stacked like sugar cubes watch you lace up. The road starts with a gentle climb and a view you will keep turning back for.

Positano was a fishing village long before it was a postcard.

Praiano 4 km

Evening in Praiano. The evening light turns the water gold here. A woman on a balcony waves your way as if she knew you were coming.

Praiano is a fishing village between Positano and Conca dei Marini.

Furore 9 km

The hidden gorge. A narrow gorge splits the cliff, with a tiny beach far below the bridge. You lean on the wall and listen to the waves echo.

Furore is often called the town that does not exist.

Conca dei Marini 13 km

Blue below. Steps lead down to a cave where the water glows. You stay up top, but the colour follows you for a while.

The Emerald Grotto takes its name from the green light inside.

Amalfi 18 km

Bells of Amalfi. A striped cathedral climbs a wide staircase above the square. The bells ring and pigeons scatter over your head.

Amalfi was a sea republic that traded across the Mediterranean.

Atrani 20 km

The smallest square. Just round the corner from Amalfi, a whole town fits in one quiet piazza. Someone hands you an espresso you did not order.

Atrani is one of the smallest towns in Italy by area.

Ravello 24 km

Gardens in the sky. You climb above the coast to terraces of flowers and a long view to the horizon. Music drifts from a concert you cannot see.

Ravello hosts a summer music festival with a stage over the sea.

Minori 30 km

Lemon country. The air smells of lemons and pastry. Terraces of fruit trees step all the way down to the beach.

The coast is known for the sfusato lemon, which is used to make limoncello.

Maiori 33 km

The long beach. For once the road runs flat beside a wide beach. Your traveller takes off their boots for a minute.

Maiori has the longest beach on the Amalfi Coast.

Cetara 41 km

The watchtower. A stone tower guards a harbour full of blue boats. Fishermen mend nets and argue happily about the weather.

Watchtowers like this once warned towns of pirates.

Vietri sul Mare 50 km

Painted tiles. The road ends among shops of bright painted ceramics. You made it the long way round.

Vietri is famous for hand-painted ceramic tiles.
