How to restart after a missed day

One missed day is a gap, not the end of a habit. What matters is how small you make the next step.

Expect gaps

Everyone misses days. Plans that assume perfect streaks tend to collapse the first time life interferes, so build gaps into the plan from the start.

Make the next session small

After a break, a full-length session can feel like a wall. Choose the Gentle rhythm of 15 minutes and promise only that. Starting is the hard part, and you can always carry on.

Use a grace day

Long Way Round counts a streak across one missed day. If you miss a day and then focus the next, the streak continues and the grace day is marked as used. It exists so that one bad day does not erase the effort around it.

Keep what you earned

The distance you walked stays walked, and stamps you have earned are never taken away. Coming back means carrying on from where you were.

Fix the record if you forgot

If you worked and forgot the timer, add the session by hand from History so your week reflects what you actually did.
