What is a virtual walk?

A virtual walk turns steady effort into steady progress along a route you could visit in real life.

The idea

A virtual walk tracks progress along a real route, such as a coastal road or a pilgrimage, without leaving your desk. People use steps from a pedometer, laps from a pool or, in Long Way Round, minutes of focus.

Why a route helps

A route has a start, an end and places in between. Progress is easy to see and each stop is a small reward. That makes a long job feel like a journey you can finish.

How Long Way Round does it

Every focus minute adds distance. The Amalfi Coast Road is 50 km over about 20 hours of focus, the Camino Portugués is 240 km and the Great Ocean Road is 243 km. At each stop you collect a postcard with a short true story and a stamp for your passport.

Not a substitute for the real thing

It is a way to make focus time feel like progress. If a road catches your imagination, the postcards might send you to visit it.
