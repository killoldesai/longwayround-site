A reading timer that keeps your page

Set your book and your page, read for a round, then log how far you got.

What the Reading area keeps

  • Your book with its title and page count, and a bookmark on your current page.
  • Pages and pace. After a session you log the page you reached. The bookmark only moves forward, so a typo cannot undo your progress.
  • Progress on Home. The plan card shows page and a progress bar for the book.

A good fit for reading

Reading is easy to put off and easy to lose track of. A timer gives you a reason to start for twenty minutes, and the page log shows how those minutes add up to a finished book.
