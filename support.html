Support

Write to killoldesai@gmail.com and say which phone and which version of the app you use. Settings shows the version at the bottom.

My session was not counted

Sessions shorter than the minimum session length in Settings are saved but add no distance. In strict mode, stopping early does not count the session. You can add or edit a session by hand from History if you forgot to start the timer.

I did not get an alert when my session ended

Allow notifications for Long Way Round in your phone's settings. Some phones also limit background alerts to save battery, so set the app to unrestricted in battery settings.

I changed phones. Where is my data?

Focus data lives on the phone it was made on. Export it from Settings as JSON before you switch. Signing in with Google keeps your account but does not yet move your sessions.

How do I switch road?

Open the Journey tab, tap Routes, and choose a road. Roads marked Pro are planned and not on sale yet.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, Account, Delete account. More detail is on the delete account page.
