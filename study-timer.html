A study timer that remembers your subject

Choose a subject, add an exam date if you have one, and study in rounds while a traveller walks the road.

What the Study area keeps

  • Subjects. Add the subjects you study. The Home screen shows the one you are on.
  • Exam dates. Optional. The plan card counts the days to your exam.
  • A method for the session. Recall, Questions, Notes or Reread, so you decide how you will study before you start.
  • An honest review log. After a session you say how it went. Shaky asks you to review tomorrow, Getting there in 3 days and Solid in a week.

Why it works with a timer

Short rounds make it easier to begin and the rest makes it easier to keep going. Pair them with a method that makes you retrieve what you learned rather than only read it again. See the guide to Pomodoro for studying.
