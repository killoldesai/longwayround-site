About Long Way Round

A small, independent app for people who want a gentler reason to keep focusing.

Most focus timers show a number going down and scold you when you stop. Long Way Round gives the number somewhere to go. Your traveller walks a real road while you focus, and every town sends a postcard.

What we care about

  • Forgiveness. Stopping early keeps your distance. A streak survives a missed day. Any session can be fixed.
  • Real places. The roads and their stories are checked against public sources, and distances are marked approximate.
  • Privacy. Your sessions stay on your phone and the app works without an account.
  • Honesty. We say what is free, what is planned and what is not built yet.
