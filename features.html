Features

Everything in the first release of Long Way Round, grouped by what it helps you do.

Time your focus

  • Three timer modes. Pomodoro, countdown and stopwatch. Read about the rhythms.
  • Rules you control. Allow pausing or not, keep going past the end with overtime, set a minimum session length, switch on strict mode, and choose whether breaks and the next round start by themselves.
  • Correct when the screen is off. The timer is worked out from the time you started, never from a running counter, so a closed app does not lose time.
  • Alerts. An alert tells you when a session or break ends.

Walk real roads

  • A traveller who walks while you focus. Pick a hiker, tortoise, owl or snail, name them, choose a colour, a hat and what they carry.
  • Three real roads with a map, stops and stories: Amalfi Coast Road, Camino Portugués and Great Ocean Road.
  • Postcards and stamps. Each stop sends a postcard with a short story and a true fact, and adds a stamp to your passport. Stamps you have earned stay earned.

Stay kind to yourself

  • Rest at the inn. Stop early and the distance you walked is kept.
  • Grace days. A streak survives one missed day.
  • Fix any session. Add, edit or delete past sessions. Deletes can be undone.
  • Weekly goal. A daily goal you set, a week view and a month view.

Focus areas that remember

  • Reading keeps your book and page. Writing tracks words. Study keeps subjects and exam dates. Work asks for one task. Language, Creative and Other keep their own stats.

Your data stays yours

  • Works offline with no account. Sessions and notes stay on your phone.
  • Export everything as CSV or JSON.
  • Google sign-in is optional. Delete your account from Settings. Privacy policy.
